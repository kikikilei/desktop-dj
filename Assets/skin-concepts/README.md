# Desktop DJ skin concepts

These 512 × 384 images are static visual references. They are not yet loaded
as selectable skins because animation and compact-headphone assets are still
required.

## States

- `playing`: the identity, palette, console, glasses, and headphone reference.
- `sleeping`: cat rests its head and folded paws on the console.
- `switching`: one paw stays near the platter while the other presses the
  upper-left performance pad.

## Ragdoll Cat

- Cream-white long colorpoint fur and fluffy tail.
- Hot-pink sports visor and metallic pink headphones.
- Pearl-white and blush-pink console with silver controls.

## Orange Tabby

- Warm orange tabby fur with white muzzle, chest, and paws.
- Red-orange cyber visor and charcoal/orange headphones.
- Matte black technological console with orange seams and red pads.
